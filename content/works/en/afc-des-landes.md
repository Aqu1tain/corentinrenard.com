## Context

The Fédération des AFC des Landes brings together two Catholic family associations: AFC Adour Océan in Dax and AFC Landes de Gascogne in Mont-de-Marsan. Their mission, uniting family love and Christian faith, lives day to day through film debates, recitals and concrete actions. That community life deserved an online home to introduce itself and announce its events.

## Approach

I built the site in PHP, in three pages: a homepage that sets the federation's identity, a news page and a contact page. The homepage moves from a "Qui sommes nous ?" section to an "Adhérer" button leading to the national AFC member portal. The news page announces upcoming gatherings, and contact goes through a deliberately simple form: name, email, message.

## Result

A restrained showcase where every visitor finds their own way in: discovering the federation, following its events, writing through the form or clicking "Adhérer". The two associations in Dax and Mont-de-Marsan now share a single online presence, true to their tone and their scale.
