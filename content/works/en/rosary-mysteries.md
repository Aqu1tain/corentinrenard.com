## Context

Rosary Mysteries is my Android app for praying the Rosary with your phone. The idea fits in one sentence: tap a mystery, read the matching Bible passage. All four sets of mysteries are there, Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious and Luminous, each tied to Scripture.

## Approach

I built it in Kotlin, native Android. Each mystery opens its Bible passage in YouVersion rather than bundling a whole bible, and the app adapts to its user: light, dark or system theme, and four languages, French, English, Spanish and Italian.

## Result

A deliberately simple app, distributed as an APK straight from the GitHub releases. No account, no needless settings: open it, pick a mystery and pray with the text in front of you.
