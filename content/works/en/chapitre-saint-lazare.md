## Context

The Chapitre Saint-Lazare is a Catholic youth movement for 15 to 22 year olds, founded in 2002 and active in Paris and Versailles. Its motto, "Connaître le Christ, et le faire connaître", comes to life in teams of about ten around four pillars: friendship, prayer, formation and action. The year runs from a summer camp abroad to a ski camp, retreats and the Pentecost pilgrimage to Chartres. I was a member of the Chapitre myself, so when a friend asked me to give it a real online presence, I knew what the site had to convey.

## Approach

The site speaks to two audiences at once: young people wondering whether to join, and parents who want to know who they are entrusting their children to. The homepage moves from the movement's calling to its pillars, its activities, upcoming events, testimonials and a practical FAQ, ending with a three-step path to join a team. The design stays restrained: midnight blue, an engraving of Chartres cathedral, and above all full-size photos from the camps. Registrations go through HelloAsso, and the contact form is shielded from spam without a captcha. Above all, I built a custom back office: the team publishes events, issues of Le Lazaréen, its newsletter, and the rest of the content on its own, without going through me.

## Result

A site that looks like the movement: young, warm and clear. Every visitor quickly finds what they came for, whether that is understanding the Chapitre, signing up for the next camp or writing to the leaders. The team runs it day to day thanks to the back office, and their verdict fits in one word: they "a-do-rent" it.
